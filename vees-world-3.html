✦  Add to Home Screen
Make it yours.
Follow the steps below to add Vee's World to your home screen so it opens like a real app.
🍎 iPhone / Safari
1
Open this page in Safari (not Chrome). If you're in another browser, copy the link and paste it in Safari.
2
Tap the Share button — the box with an arrow pointing up ↑ — at the bottom of Safari.
3
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4
Name it Vee's World and tap Add. Done — the 🪼 icon will appear on your home screen!
🤖 Android / Chrome
1
Open this page in Chrome.
2
Tap the three dots ⋮ menu in the top right corner.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App".
4
Confirm and the 🪼 icon will be on your home screen!
✦  Note: To use the app offline you'll need to save this HTML file locally and open it from there, or host it free on Netlify or GitHub Pages.
